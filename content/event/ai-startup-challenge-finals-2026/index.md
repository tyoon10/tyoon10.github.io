As **AI Club leader at Columbia Business School**, I co-organized the school's flagship AI startup pitch competition — the AI Startup Challenge — in partnership with Columbia's AI in Business Initiative.

## Role

Co-led planning and execution of the final competition day alongside the AI in Business Initiative: team selection, judging panel, sponsor coordination, and event programming. The Challenge is the AI Club's signature annual showcase, surfacing the most ambitious student-built AI startups across the New York university ecosystem.

## Format

A multi-university final bringing together teams from **Columbia, Cornell Tech, and Yale** to pitch AI-native startups to a panel of investors, founders, and operators. The day combined pitch rounds with a fireside conversation on building and scaling AI companies, moderated by **Professor Mattan Griffel**.

## Why It Matters

The Challenge is more than a pitch day — it's the AI Club's way of staking out Columbia Business School as a serious home for AI founders, on a stage shared with the strongest technical programs in the region. Running it as a co-organizer meant turning that ambition into logistics: which teams advance, which judges show up, which sponsors back it, and what story the day tells about CBS in the AI era.
