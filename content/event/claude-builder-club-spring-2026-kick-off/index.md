## Frequently Asked Questions

### When do I get the free Claude Pro subscription?

The Anthropic team processes access codes in batches every Friday. After submitting the check-in form, you'll receive an email with your Claude Pro access code. Please allow up to one week for processing.

### How do I sign up for CBC?

Use the [CBC mailing list sign-up link](https://form.jotform.com/253555944387168) to join and stay updated on upcoming events.

### What's the difference between claude.ai and platform.claude.com? Which one do I use for the check-in form?

- **claude.ai** — This is the consumer chat interface. You do *not* need the Org ID from here.
- **platform.claude.com** — This is the Anthropic API console (developer platform). You should get your **Org ID from platform.claude.com** to fill out the check-in form.

### Which email address can I use?

You must use a **.edu school email** — either `@columbia.edu` or `@gsb.columbia.edu`.

### How do I find my Org ID?

1. Go to [platform.claude.com](https://platform.claude.com)
2. Create an account using your school email (`.edu`)
3. When prompted, create an **Individual Org**
4. Once logged in, click **Settings** at the bottom of the left sidebar
5. Your **Org ID** will be displayed on the settings page — copy and paste it into the check-in form

### I've been waiting over a week and still haven't received my Claude Pro access code

Try these troubleshooting steps:

1. **Check your inbox and spam/junk folder** for an email from Anthropic
2. **Verify the information you submitted** — the most common mistake is entering your **claude.ai** Org ID instead of your **platform.claude.com** Org ID
3. **Resubmit the [check-in form](https://www.jotform.com/253566966596075)** with the corrected Org ID from platform.claude.com
4. **Wait one more week** for the next Friday batch processing
5. If you're still not receiving anything after resubmitting, **please join the next CBC event** so we can help troubleshoot in person
