One Sunday. 200 builders. Ship something real with Claude.

Co-hosted the **Claude Builder Hackathon** at Columbia Business School's David Geffen Hall — a one-day sprint bringing together students from Columbia, NYU, and across NYC to build real products with **Claude, the Claude Agent SDK, and Claude Code**. 283 registered, rooms packed, and final demos ran on the main stage.

## Role

Co-organizer alongside **Ian Fong, Sophie Zhang, YiLin Choi, and Justin Kim** — running logistics, kickoff, judging criteria, and finalist demos. Hosted by the **Columbia Claude Builder Club**, **NYU Claude Builder Club**, and the **CBS AI Club**.

## What Happened

- **200+ builders** from Columbia, NYU, and across NYC — technical and non-technical, undergrad and grad
- **$3,000 in Anthropic credits + cash prizes** across the Grand Prize and sponsor challenges
- **$25 Claude API credits** for eligible Columbia / NYU students, plus Claude merch and rolling mentorship
- Live office hours with **Anthropic**, **Linkup**, and Claude Ambassadors throughout the day
- Finalist demos in room 590, judged by big-tech technologists and founders

## Tracks & Challenges

- **Grand Prize** — Best overall build with Claude
- **Linkup Challenge** — Best use of the Linkup search API

All winning teams received Anthropic API credits to keep building after the hackathon.

## Judging Criteria

1. **Problem Significance** — well-defined and highly impactful problem
2. **Creativity & Feasibility** — original approach, technically functional and sound
3. **Long-term Potential** — solutions that scale and continue beyond the hackathon
4. **AI Integration** — how deeply and effectively Claude is integrated into the core system
