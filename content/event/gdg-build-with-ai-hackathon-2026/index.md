Selected as a hackathon mentor for Google Developer Groups NYC's second Build With AI Hackathon — a full-day event hosted at Columbia Business School in partnership with **Google Cloud Labs** and the **AI Futures Fund**.

## Role

Invited to mentor teams building AI-powered applications on Google Cloud and Gemini, providing guidance on technical architecture, product strategy, and real-world applicability. One of a small group of mentors selected to guide 550+ registered participants through rapid prototyping and demo preparation.

## Scale & Significance

Over **550 registrants** (428+ confirmed) for an intensive day of building at Columbia Business School. The event is part of Google's global Build With AI hackathon series, organized locally by GDG NYC — one of the largest Google Developer Groups chapters in the world.

## Partners & Sponsors

**Google Cloud Labs** | **AI Futures Fund** | **Google Developer Groups NYC**

## Context

This continues a month-long arc across New York's AI builder community — from co-organizing the [AI Club x Iterate NYC Hackathon](/event/iterate-columbia-hackathon-2026/) (300 builders) and [judging Mistral's worldwide hackathon](/event/mistral-worldwide-hackathon-2026/) (1,000+ engineers across 7 cities), to now mentoring at Google's flagship NYC hackathon. Three major hackathons in four weeks, each with a different role: organizer, judge, mentor.
