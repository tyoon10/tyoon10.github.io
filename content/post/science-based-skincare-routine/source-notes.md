## 3. Sunlight

low-angle sunlight exposure
- wake up 10 min before local sunrise time
- immediately after wake up -> go for a walk to a nearby park to watch a sunrise
- 5-10 min low angle sunlight exposure : red and near-infrared light - natural photobiomodulation (similar to the red-light therapy panels in dermatology)
- after moving recently, I go to a rooftop where I can get an unobstructed view

sunscreen
- MUST if total direct exposure for the entire day is >20 min
- regardless of weather (overcast and cloudy)

Product: 자작나무 수분 선크림
note: the Korean version vs the US version differ in UV filter technology (given SPF 50+ formula unapproved by US FDA); I prefer Korean version (superior filter technology)
