## Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with the content. GA4 collects:

- Pages visited and time spent
- Referral source (e.g., LinkedIn, direct)
- General geographic region (country/city level)
- Device type and browser

**What we do NOT collect:**
- Personally identifiable information
- IP addresses (anonymized by default in GA4)

**Your choices:**
- If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal, analytics are disabled automatically.
- You can also block tracking by using a browser extension such as [uBlock Origin](https://ublockorigin.com/) or [Google's opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
