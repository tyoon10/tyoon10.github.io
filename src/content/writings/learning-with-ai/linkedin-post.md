# LinkedIn Post: How I Learn Anything with AI

I'm publishing my entire AI chat transcript. Here's how you learn anything with AI 💡

Three days before my Causal Inference midterm. Six weeks of untouched material. A subject that resists memorization, with its own mathematical framework, graphical language, and methods that each break in different ways.

I opened Claude and started arguing. Here's everything that came out of it:

**1. The entire chat transcript.**

Not a polished summary. The raw back-and-forth: wrong turns, corrections, moments where I thought I understood something and didn't. Every time Claude introduced a new method, I refused to move on until I could explain it back in concrete terms. 

You can read every exchange, steal whatever is useful, or see exactly where my thinking broke.

**2. A diagnostic of my reasoning patterns.**

I asked Claude to analyze how I learn, not what I was learning. 

What it got right: "Your learning style is adversarial-constructive. You don't passively receive, you probe, challenge, and test." 

What it flagged: when I correctly ruled something out, I was treating that elimination as the complete answer instead of continuing to search. And my synthesis attempts were describing *what* but not always *why*. That gap between "what does this do?" and "why does this work?" is where I was leaving points on the table.

**3. An interactive study module.**

While arguing, I was building. 

A single-file HTML application with visualizations for every major concept. The propensity score overlap viz came from questioning the support assumption. The Back-Door Adjustment animation came from struggling with why averaging implements a specific weighting. 

Each module was born from a SPECIFIC MOMENT where my understanding broke. None were single-prompt outputs.

This is the same core method that got me through CFA II and III in under two weeks each, back in 2018 before any of these tools existed. AI didn't change the method. It collapsed the feedback loop from days to seconds.

This is how you LEARN ANYTHING with AI:
Progressive synthesis. Immediate feedback. Build while you learn.
